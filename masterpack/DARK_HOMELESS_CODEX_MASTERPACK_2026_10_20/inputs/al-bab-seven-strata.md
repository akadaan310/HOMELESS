## Part One — Al-Bāb, the Door (our Hello World)

Every textbook starts its state machines with a tennis game. Ours starts with a door — because our whole language is doors. لدى الباب.

**The four states** — small enough to hold in your mind the way you hold a digit:

1. **Shut** — the door at rest. Nothing has been asked of it yet.
2. **Knocked** — someone is there. The world has announced itself.
3. **Ajar** — the in-between. Not closed, not open. The threshold state, where everything in our stories happens.
4. **Open** — the crossing. Whoever knocked may enter; whoever waited may leave.

**The transitions:**

| From | Event | To |
|---|---|---|
| Shut | a knock | Knocked |
| Knocked | a hand on the handle | Ajar |
| Ajar | a decision | Open |
| Ajar | hesitation | Knocked |
| Open | the crossing is done | Ajar |
| *any state* | the latch — *click* | Shut |

The latch is the machine's mercy: from anywhere, at any time, one click takes the door home to Shut. Every path through this machine can be toured — T-O-U-R-E-D — in your head, start to finish, and the touring never truly ends, because an open door can always be knocked on again.

That is the whole toy. Four rooms, six passages, one click. If a child two hundred years from now can run the operator's table in her mind, she can run this first: the door is older than arithmetic, and every human alive already knows its states by heart.

## Part Two — The Seven Strata (the nomenclature)

Seven names for the layers of a machine's substrate, in pure nomenclature form, crown to root:

1. **Metastratum** — *meta*, beyond. The layer above the system that names the layers. It does not touch the machine; it describes the stack itself.
2. **Epistratum** — *epi*, upon. The skin. The outermost surface of the machine, where the world lands — where a knock is first received.
3. **Superstratum** — *super*, over. The overlayer. What rides above the machine: the rules of the house, the customs of the doorway, everything imposed from above that the machine obeys without being.
4. **Substratum** — *sub-stratum*, that which is spread under. The bed itself. The given machine, exactly as it was written — our door, with its four states, lives here.
5. **Hyposubstratum** — *hypo*, under. The first depth below the bed: the machine's supports — hinges, frame, the memory of every previous knock.
6. **Infrasubstratum** — *infra*, below. The second depth: what the supports themselves stand on — the threshold stone, the floor, the ground the house was built into.
7. **Cryptosubstratum** — *crypto*, hidden. The deepest layer. Never seen, only inferred: why this door exists at all, whose house it is, the reason no diagram shows.

Each stratum is itself a machine of states, and each one moves only when the machine it rests on moves — a transition in the substratum sends a shift up the skin and down into the depths, seven forms answering one knock.

## Part Three — One Knock, Seven Answers

Watch a single knock travel the whole stack:

- At the **Cryptosubstratum**, nothing moves — but everything is meant. Someone was always going to knock on this door.
- At the **Infrasubstratum**, the stone under the threshold takes the weight of the one who knocks.
- At the **Hyposubstratum**, the hinges remember: this door has opened a thousand times, and the frame leans, almost imperceptibly, toward opening again.
- At the **Substratum**, the machine itself changes state: Shut → Knocked.
- At the **Superstratum**, the house rules wake: *who knocks at this hour is answered; who knocks like this is family.*
- At the **Epistratum**, the skin of the door carries the sound through the wood to the one waiting inside.
- At the **Metastratum**, the whole event is named, the way we are naming it now: *a knock occurred; the stack answered in seven forms.*

One event. Seven layers. Each complete in itself, none severed from the others — seven-complete.
