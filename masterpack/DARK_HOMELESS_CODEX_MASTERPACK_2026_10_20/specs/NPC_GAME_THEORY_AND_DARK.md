# DARK · SOCIETY · NPC LIFE · GAME THEORY · COAUTHORSHIP

## Identity

Dark = the founder Abed Kadan's authentic in-game identity and optional avatar. Other players may join a **Dark Cohort**, with opt-in attribution, badges, revocable capabilities and strict differentiation between actual founder actions, a delegated member and an NPC using a story role. Founder may operate an all-world observatory and staging interface without forcing other human sessions into an unannounced mission. First new member may arrive when no other human is online; simulated NPC world is already rich.

## Authoritative NPC record

`Actor=(actor_id,world_cell,role,appearance,culture/language_metadata,goals,needs,preferences,knowledge_distribution,beliefs,schedule,relationships,capabilities,policies,memory/events,autonomy_budget,render_LOD)`. No claim of real consciousness: subjective intent is a **modeled** decision structure. NPCs do not know every global fact; rumor, awareness, trust and observation have separate types. Economy and income are numerical context only, never deterministically personality or moral worth. Synthetic actor identities are distinct from real individuals.

## Deterministic game-theory basis

For each decision, enumerate admissible `A_i(s)` and known/believed state; record `U_i(a_i,a_-i;s,beliefs)`, constraints, chosen solution concept (best response, minimax, Pareto selection, correlated-equilibrium approximation, repeated-game planning), solver/seed and explicit tie-break. Local NPC policies minimize simulation costs using cached or compiled deterministic tactics but retain intent and refusal. Deliberate stochasticity is seeded and replayable. Do not confuse game theory with random narrative templates.

**Example taxi interaction**: Dark asks an autonomous NPC rider about shared ride; NPC may accept if destination compatibility, seat space, confidence/relationship and their available time support it. If not, NPC declines. Vehicle authorization and drive handoff require explicit approval from the current fictional operator. No uninvited physical takeover. Entourage mode coordinates consenting fictional cars through a bounded route-formation planner, obeying simulated safe spacing and no emergency-lane priority claims.

## Actions are multiscale world events

Player action `walk`, `pause`, `notice`, `look_up`, `speak`, `gesture`, `enter_vehicle`, `accept_ride`, `observe_sign`, `recognize_pattern`, `save_skill`, `propose_name`, `join_cohort` produces a typed candidate event. At each simulation tick, activated nearby actors, regional cohorts and persistent world-state records react based on actual knowledge and causal relationships. Effects distribute with a declared propagation matrix, weighted graph and saturation/decay; distant NPCs not causally affected do not react by fiat. Rate limit large cascades and preserve independent agency. Event distributions come from source domain constraints and transparent candidate generators.

## Real in-world authored shops

Actual business names are not shown by default. Engine constructs fictional display name from local history, morphology, scene class, phonetic and semantic dictionaries, with a seeded grammar and provenance. A scene's storefront, label, mural, cafe identity or billboard can be renamed through social coauthorship. Store's role/class is preserved under the real-world geographic association but the in-game real/factual identity is not claimed. Proposals recorded with supporter/opponent choices and developer moderation control. Participation grants identity stamps and reversible creative contributions, not control over real assets.

## Pattern school and seal economy

An actor who discovers a new verified `walk signature ↔ remote chart` correspondence receives an addressable *seal*: normalized signature, source witness, rarity, epoch if sky-linked, certificate hash and authorship metadata. A seal may be taught to a voluntary NPC, traded through a fictional exchange, included in a cooperative mission or unfolded into a journey program. Reuse can be gated by world capability checks. A common seal is not worthless; smaller impacts remain visible in personal world history.

## Mission grammar

`Quest := Observation → Offer | Discover → Consent? → Attempt* → Outcome → Witness`.

All participants retain opt-in or refusal; a story about corruption/coercion can conclude with *fictional* dismissal, removal from power, exposure, escape or relocation. Tension comes from information, alliances, timed opportunities, conversations and controlled symbolic performances, not actionable guidance for real-world harm. A staged possession-trade/misunderstanding may look like a theft trope, but the underlying scripted asset transfer uses permission recorded in world rules; don't design tutorials for avoiding real cameras, stealing property or harming real people. The world can reveal to a player afterward that the fictional object's transfer had been negotiated in advance. No weapons required for player advancement.

## Moving social environment

Every NPC has camera-independent locomotion policy. Groups walk together or split when goals differ; actors naturally wait, make way or converse. City sound, park/beach/market gatherings, transportation, families, food carts, scooters and airports run independently of specific missions. Walkers and drivers notice one another according to plausible orientation and attention budgets. Sudden global synchronized reactions are restricted to the authored onboarding event and visually scaled with distance and social dynamics.

## Governance and player creation

Authorship object `{proposal_id,source_grammars,author,collaborators,scene_id,scope,details,endorsements,declines,adoption_event,revision,unfold_witness}`. Nonconsenting collaborators are not credited falsely. Creative stamps and code programs become discoverable in-game and optionally exportable; internal shell execution privileges are not earned automatically from a cosmetic badge. Dark can inspect and approve contributions in the world-control view.

## Measurable requirements

1. Same replay of seeded NPC encounters returns same dialogue-act, action policy and causality hash given pinned model versions.
2. In a ride-negotiation matrix fixture, at least two equilibria yield different outcomes and chosen tie-break is recorded.
3. `NPC declines`, `NPC does not notice`, `NPC teaches`, `NPC coauthors`, `NPC notices sky cue` are distinct tested paths.
4. NPC interaction triggered when world line-of-sight/voice/proximity verifies plausible reach; no omniscient cross-city NPC awareness without a declared communication event.
5. Return after a game-clock interval demonstrates persisted consequences, realistic schedules and event deduplication.
6. Dark's founder badge/authority cannot be impersonated by NPC or player-created script. Cohort membership requires voluntary approval and can be revoked.
