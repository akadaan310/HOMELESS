# Counterexamples and unaccepted requirements

The commission is not fully accepted. [evidence/acceptance.json](evidence/acceptance.json) records every original check, all twelve V3 tasks and additive V1/V2 gates with thresholds, procedures, evidence hashes and blockers. [evidence/acceptance.md](evidence/acceptance.md) is the compact ledger. Regenerate after final builds/tests with `python tools/build_acceptance.py --test-log evidence/final-python-tests.log --test-log evidence/final-node-tests.log`.

The following are executed negative results, not hypothetical risks:

- Arabic ASR changes one of three normalized words: WER 0.333. English clean WER 0 does not establish multilingual release quality. Synthetic 10dB noisy English also produces WER 0.125. See [voice_benchmarks.json](evidence/voice_benchmarks.json).
- Kokoro int8 takes 6.86 and 3.77 times the generated audio duration on the local CPU. This fixture does not support real-time synthesis. See [voice_kokoro_benchmarks.json](evidence/voice_kokoro_benchmarks.json).
- Matching pennies has no pure equilibrium. The bounded pure-strategy solver cannot promise an agreement or solve mixed strategies. The camera's reduced jerk also leaves normalized tracking error near 0.333. See [math_benchmarks.json](evidence/math_benchmarks.json) and [math_findings.jsonl](evidence/math_findings.jsonl).
- Tampa–London and Tampa–Marina route winners have held-out p=0.39535 and 0.76744 against α=0.05. Both are valid tested negatives; neither is certified rare correspondence. See [London](evidence/geo_match_tampa_london.json) and [Marina](evidence/geo_match_tampa_marina.json).
- The sky motif has held-out p=0.17, matches three of four Southern Cross vertices and one of two traditional edges. Partial source-star agreement does not recover the traditional drawing or establish rarity. See [sky_discovery.json](evidence/sky_discovery.json).
